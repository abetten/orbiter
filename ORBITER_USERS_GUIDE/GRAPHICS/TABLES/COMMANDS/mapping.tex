\hline
\verb'-ring' & R & Set the coordinate ring for D. \\
\hline
\verb'-formula' & f & Use the symbolic object f to define the mapping. \\
\hline
\verb'-substitute' & s & Substitute s into the equation before doing the mapping. \\
\hline
\verb'-affine' &  & Treat the domain as an affine space. \\
\hline
\verb'-object_in_codomain_cubic_surface' & S & 
Set the codomain to the cubic surface object S. 
The map will now output images as indices of points on the surface. \\
