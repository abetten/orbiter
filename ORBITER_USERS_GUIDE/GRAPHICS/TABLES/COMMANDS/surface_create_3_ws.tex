Command & Arguments & Purpose \\
\hline
\hline
\verb'-Cayley_form'  &  $k$ $l$ $m$ $n$    & Create the surface from Cayley's normal form, using the parameters $k,l,m,n.$\\
\hline
\verb'-Pentahedral_form'  &  params25    & Create the surface from Sylvester's pentahedral normal form, using the give 25 parameters. The first 20 parameters are 5 sets of 4 coefficients each of planes, the latter 5 parameters are the coefficients of the linear combination of the third powers of the five planes. \\
\hline
\verb'-by_equation'  &  R label label\_tex  vars eqn para para\_tex para\_values  & Create the surface from an equation.\\
\hline
\verb'-by_symbolic_object'  &   R formula   & Create the surface from an equation. Here, R is a ring and formula is the equation of the surface. \\
\hline
\verb'-by_double_six'  &   label label\_tex $D$   & Create the surface from a double six $D$.\\
\hline
\verb'-by_skew_hexagon'  &   label label   &  Create the surface from a skew hexagon.\\
\hline
\verb'-select_double_six'  &   $L$    & Establish a new set of Schlaefli labels by distinguishing a double six, using an existing Schlaefli labeling. The double six is given in $L$, and consists of 12 comma separated entries (first the six $a_i$, then the corresponding six $b_j$). The entries in $L$ can be either Schlaefli symbols or numerical line indices. The latter are integers in the interval $[0,26].$ This command can be repeated. In each application, the selection takes place with respect to the previous Schlaefli labeling. \\
\hline
\verb'-override_group'  &  descr  & Override the automorphism group of the surface by the given group. \\
\hline
\verb'-random'  &      & Create a random cubic surface with 27 lines over the given field.\\
\hline
\verb'-transform'  &  elt  &  Apply the transformation given by the group element. \\
\hline
\verb'-transform_inverse'  &  elt  &  Apply the inverse transformation given by the group element. \\
