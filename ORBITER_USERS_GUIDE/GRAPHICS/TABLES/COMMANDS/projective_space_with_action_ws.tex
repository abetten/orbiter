Command & Arguments & Purpose \\

\hline
\verb'-n'  &  $n$  &   Specify the projective dimension $n$. \\
\hline
\verb'-q'  &  $q$  &   Specify the order $q$ of the underlying finite field $\bbF_q$. A default version of the field $\bbF_q$ will be created. \\
\hline
\verb'-field'  &  F  &   Specify the underlying field $F$.  \\
\hline
\verb'-use_projectivity_subgroup'  &   &  Create the projectivity group instead of the collineation group. \\
\hline
\verb'-v'  &   $k$  & Set verbosity to $k$ during creation of the projective space. Higher values of $k$ give more output. $k=0$ is silent mode.\\
