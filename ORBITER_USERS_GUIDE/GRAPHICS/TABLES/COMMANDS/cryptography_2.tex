		\verb'-EC_Koblitz_encoding' & $a$ $b$ $s$ pt plain  & Computes the Koblitz encoding of ``plain'' (all caps) on the elliptic curve $y^2\equiv x^3 + ax+b$ over $\bbF_q$ using the base point pt and the secret exponent $s$ \\

		\verb'-EC_bsgs_decode' & $a$ $b$ pt $n$ cipher round-keys & Decodes the ciphertext ``cipher'' on the elliptic curve $y^2\equiv x^3 + ax+b$ over $\bbF_q$ using the base point pt of order $n$ and the round keys ``keys''\\
