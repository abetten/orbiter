Command & Arguments & Purpose \\
\hline
\hline
\verb'-V'  &  part   & The initial partition of points (rows). \\
\hline
\verb'-B'  &  part  & The initial partition of blocks (columns). \\
\hline
\verb'-TDO'  &  tdo  & The initial row-tactical decomposition scheme. \\
\hline
\verb'-fuse'  &  fuse  & The partition of row classes. \\
\hline
\verb'-girth_test'  &  $g$  & Require the girth of the collinearity graph to be at least $g$. \\
\hline
\verb'-lambda'  &  $\lambda$  & Set $\lambda$ for two-design test. Every pair of points is required to lie in $\lambda$ blocks. \\
\hline
\verb'-find_square'  &    & Enable the test for linear spaces. \\
\hline
\verb'-simple'  &    & Construct simple designs only (needs \verb'-lambda') \\
\hline
\verb'-search_tree'  &    & Write a file containing the search tree (at the level of rows of the partial geometry). \\
\hline
\verb'-search_tree_flags'  &    & Write a file containing the search tree (at the level of flags of the partial geometry). A flag is an incident point-block pair. \\
\hline
\verb'-orderly'  &    & Apply orderly generation. \\
\hline
\verb'-special_test_not_orderly'  &    & Apply the special test. This option only applies to generation which is not orderly. \\
\hline
\verb'-test_lines'  &  lines  & Install isomorphism checking at the given set of lines. Lines must be an object of type vector or set. \\
\hline
\verb'-test2_lines'  &  lines  & Install isomorphism checking of the second type at the given set of lines. Lines must be an object of type vector or set. \\
\hline
\verb'-split'  &  l r m  & At line l, proceed only with the cases whose number is congruent to r modulo m. This allows splitting the search tree into smaller subtrees. \\
\hline
\verb'-print_at_line'  &  lines  & Print the geometries considered at the given set of lines.\\
\hline
\verb'-fname_GEO'  &  fname  & Set the output file name base (no extension). \\
\hline
\verb'-output_to_inc_file'  &    & Set output to inc file.  \\
\hline
\verb'-output_to_sage_file'  &    & Set output to sage file. \\
\hline
\verb'-output_to_blocks_file'  &    & Set output to a file containing the blocks in coded form. \\
\hline
\verb'-output_to_blocks_latex_file'  &    & Set output to a file containing the blocks in latex. \\
\hline
\verb'-save_canonical_forms'  &    & Saves the canonical forms to a csv file. \\
\hline
