		\hline

		Computes the partition of the field elements 
		according to the value of their absolute norm.  \\
		\hline
		\verb'-normal_basis' & $d$  &  
		Computes a normal basis for $\bbF_{q^d}$ over $\bbF_q.$  \\
		\hline
		\verb'-nth_roots' & $n$  &  
		Compute the minimum polynomials of all $n$-th roots over $F$.  \\
		\hline
		\verb'-field_reduction' & label $q$ $m$ $n$ $v$ &  
		Compute the field reduction from $F$ to the subfield $\bbF_q$ 
		of the $m \times n$ matrix whose coefficients are given in $v$.  \\
