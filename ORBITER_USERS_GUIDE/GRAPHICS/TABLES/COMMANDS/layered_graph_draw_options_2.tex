Command & Arguments & Purpose \\
\hline
\verb'-scale' & $s$  & 
Use Tikz global scale-factor of $s$. 
Default value: $s=0.45$.\\
\hline
\verb'-line_width' & $s$  & 
Set Tikz line width to $s$. 
Default value: $s=1.5$. \\
\hline
\verb'-rotated' &   & 
Rotate the output.  \\
\hline
\verb'-nodes' &   & 
Turn on node drawing.  \\
\hline
\verb'-nodes_empty' &   & 
Do not label the nodes. 
Default value: off.\\
\hline
\verb'-select_layers' &  $S$ & 
Draw layers whose index is given in the list $S$ only. \\
\hline
\verb'-paths_in_between' & $l_1$ $i_1$ $l_2$ $i_2$ & 
Draw all paths from node $(l_1,i_1)$ to node $(l_2,i_2)$. 
Here, $(l,i)$ is the $i$-th node at layer $l$ (counting from zero). 
Delete all other edges between layers $l_1$ and $l_2.$ \\
\hline
