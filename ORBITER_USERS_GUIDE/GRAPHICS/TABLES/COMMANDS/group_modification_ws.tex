Command & Arguments & Purpose \\
\hline
\hline
\verb'-restricted_action'  &  $S$ label-tex  &  restricted action on the set $S$ using the given tex-label. \\
\hline
\verb'-on_k_subspaces'  &  $k$  &  induced action on $k$ dimensional subspaces \\
\hline
\verb'-on_k_subsets'  &  $k$  &  induced action on $k$ subsets. \\
\hline
\verb'-on_wedge_product'  &    &  action on the exterior square \\
\hline
\verb'-on_cosets_of_subgroup'  &  H  &  Action on the codes of $H$ in the given group. $H$ must be a subgroup of the given group. \\
\hline
\verb'-create_special_subgroup'  &    &  Create the subgroup of special matrices. \\
\hline
\verb'-create_even_subgroup'  &    &  Create the subgroup of even permutations. \\
\hline
\verb'-derived_subgroup'  &    &  Create the derived subgroup. \\
\hline
\verb'-point_stabilizer'  &  pt  &  Create the subgroup which stabilizes pt. \\
\hline
\verb'-set_stabilizer'  &  set control  &  Create the subgroup which stabilizes the given set. Requires a poset classification control object to generate the orbits on subsets. \\
\hline
\verb'-projectivity_subgroup'  &    &  Create the projectivity subgroup of a semilinear group. \\
\hline
\verb'-field_reduction'  &   &  Create the field reduced group. \\
\hline
\verb'-action_on_self_by_' \verb'right_multiplication'  &   &  Create the action on the group itself by right multiplication. \\
\hline
\verb'-action_on_self_by_conjugation'  &   &  Create the action on the group itself by conjugation. \\
\hline
\verb'-direct_product'  &  list order gens  &  Create the direct product action of the two actions given in list (comma separated). Then create the subgroup of the given order from the given generators. \\
\hline
\verb'-polarity_extension'  &  G0 P  &  Create the polarity extension of G0. P is the underlying projective space on which G0 acts. \\
\hline
\verb'-automorphism_group'  &    &  Create the automorphism group of the input group (which is given in \verb'-from'). This command requires Magma. \\
\hline
\verb'-subgroup_by_lattice'  &  i  &  Create the representative of the $i$-th conjugacy class of subgroups in the lattice of subgroups. The conjugacy classes of subgroups must have been computed before using the group theoretic activity \verb'-subgroup_lattice_magma'. A shortened base will be computed for the subgroup. The shortened base contains only those base points for which the basic orbit is non-trivial. See Section~\ref{sec:lineargroups:advanced:topics}. \\
\hline
\verb'-stabilizer_of_variety'  &  variety  &  Create the stabilizer of the given variety. \\
\hline
\verb'-subgroup_by_generators'  &  elements  &  Create the subgroup generated by the given set of elements. \\
\hline
\verb'-nauty_control'  &   options  &  Nauty options, see Table~\ref{tab:nauty:control}.  \\
\hline
\verb'-from'  &  label  &  Specify the input group action (this option can be repeated). \\
