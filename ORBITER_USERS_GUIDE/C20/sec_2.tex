Orbiter contains a library of BLT-sets.
It is possible to export data on BLT-sets to csv-files. 
For instance, 
the command 

\sourcecode{blt_set_table_q13}

exports the BLT-sets over the field $\bbF_{13}$ 
contained in the Orbiter knowledge base.

\bigskip

The command 
\sourcecode{blt_set_table}
creates tables of BLT-sets for all orders up to 31.


\bigskip

The command 
\sourcecodevariable{BLT_ORDER_Q}
\sourcecodevariable{BLT_NUMBER_ISO}
\sourcecode{blt_set_export_gap}
exports all BLT-sets of order at most 73 to GAP.
